What this does — and what it never touches
  • Moves GRNs, every supplier payment (active and voided), the opening balance, legacy stock rows, linked accounts and manual cash-flow rows to the receiving supplier.
  • Re-syncs the Accounts section in the same transaction: supplier payment rows, cash/bank transactions, the linked-account widgets and the cash-flow page all follow the move.
  • Leaves inventory quantities, material totals, GRN lots, FIFO allocations and customer sales exactly as they are.
  • Is reversible: after the shift, press Reclaim on the emptied supplier to move its ledger back.
